Worship Translator

AI-Powered Real-time Audio & Text Translation

A live AI translation tool designed specifically for worship services. Translates sermons into 12+ languages simultaneously via high-quality Speech-to-Text and AI Text-to-Speech narration, integrating biblical terminology from standard Bible translations (VIE2010, NIV, etc.).

Open Live App
  • Multi-lingual Support: Vietnamese, English, Korean, Chinese, Thai, Bahasa, French, German, and more.
  • QR Code Listener Access: Congregation members scan QR code to listen or read live on mobile web browsers.
  • Biblical Context Engine: Context-aware prompts tailored for sermon vocabulary and scripture citations.

VEC Church Management System (ChMS)

Multi-tenant Cloud Platform for Church Operations

An enterprise-grade Church Management Platform built with Django REST Framework, Angular/React, and Cloud SQL. Supports multi-tenant church branches, believer databases, Sunday school attendance, pastoral care visits, financial reports, and election voting.

  • Believer & Family Directory: Comprehensive member profiling, household trees, area/cell group assignments.
  • Sunday School & Classes: Automated attendance tracking, trainer management, lesson logs.
  • Pastoral Visitation & Elections: Schedule pastoral care visits and execute transparent church board elections.
